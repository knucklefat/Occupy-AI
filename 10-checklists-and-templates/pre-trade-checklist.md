# Pre-Trade Checklist

**In one sentence:** Twenty questions to answer in writing — taking about ten minutes — before every buy or sell that isn't a scheduled contribution or rebalance, because the trades that hurt most are the ones made in the thirty seconds between a feeling and a tap.

Scheduled contributions and rule-based rebalancing don't need this checklist; your IPS already made those decisions. Everything else does: buying an individual stock, selling a fund because it "isn't working," switching strategies, adding a new asset class, acting on news. Keep a running log of completed checklists; reviewing it a year later is the best investing education available at no cost.

**The one-line version, if you only do one thing:** *Would I make this exact trade if I hadn't seen anything in the news, on social media, or in my account balance in the last week?*

---

## Section 1 — The thesis

- [ ] 1. **In one sentence, why am I doing this?** "I am [buying/selling] [X] because [specific reason]." If the sentence contains "feels," "everyone," "might," or "before it's too late," stop.
  *Example:* "I am buying $4,000 of XYZ because it trades at 12× free cash flow with net cash and a 10-year record of 8% growth, and my full research checklist passed."
- [ ] 2. **What specifically would prove me wrong?** Name two or three observable facts (a metric, an event, a price/valuation level). If nothing could prove me wrong, this is a belief, not a thesis.
- [ ] 3. **What is my time horizon for this trade?** [Days / months / years]. If it's less than a year, I'm trading, not investing — is trading in my IPS?
- [ ] 4. **What return do I expect, and over what period?** A number. "Up" is not a number.
- [ ] 5. **Who is on the other side, and what do they know?** For every buyer there's a seller who looked at the same price and chose the opposite. Why are they wrong?

## Section 2 — Is this in my plan?

- [ ] 6. **Is this trade permitted by my IPS?** Asset class, single-position limit, speculative cap, "never" list. Quote the relevant line. If it's not permitted, the trade doesn't happen — the IPS can be changed, in writing, after a 30-day wait.
- [ ] 7. **What does the new allocation look like after this trade?** Still within every band? Sector or single-name concentration still acceptable?
- [ ] 8. **Have I completed the relevant research checklist?** [stock-research-checklist.md](stock-research-checklist.md) for a stock; [etf-and-fund-selection-checklist.md](etf-and-fund-selection-checklist.md) for a fund. When did I last update it?

## Section 3 — Size and mechanics

- [ ] 9. **Position size as a percent of total portfolio (not of this account):** [__%]. If it's the maximum my IPS allows, why does this idea deserve the maximum?
- [ ] 10. **Could I lose all of it without changing a single life plan?** If not, it's too big.
- [ ] 11. **All at once or in tranches?** Decided and written down, with the trigger for each tranche.
- [ ] 12. **Which account, and why?** Taxable vs Roth vs Traditional. Anything I might trade or that pays high income belongs in a tax-advantaged account.
- [ ] 13. **Order type:** limit orders for anything but the largest, most liquid ETFs; never a market order in the first or last 15 minutes of trading or in a thinly traded security.

## Section 4 — Tax impact (taxable accounts)

- [ ] 14. **If selling: what is the gain or loss, and is it short-term (held ≤ 1 year, taxed as income) or long-term?** Would waiting a few weeks convert short-term to long-term?
- [ ] 15. **Which tax lots am I selling?** Specific-lot identification (highest cost first) rather than the broker's default (often first-in-first-out).
- [ ] 16. **Wash-sale check:** if realizing a loss, have I bought (or will I buy) the same or substantially identical security in *any* account — including IRAs and automatic dividend reinvestment — within 30 days on either side?
- [ ] 17. **Does this sale affect anything income-linked** this year — ACA subsidies, IRMAA two years out, the 0% capital-gains bracket, financial aid?

## Section 5 — Emotion and information audit

*Why this matters:* Nearly every documented investor mistake — chasing performance, panic selling, over-trading, overconfidence — begins as an emotion dressed up as analysis. The purpose of this section is to name it.

- [ ] 18. **What prompted this trade?** Circle one: scheduled review / new research / a price move / a news story / a social media post / someone I know / a feeling about my balance. Only the first two are legitimate triggers. The rest require a 72-hour wait.
- [ ] 19. **How do I feel right now?** Fear of missing out, fear of loss, boredom, revenge for a prior loss, wanting to feel smart, wanting to "do something." Any of these present? A trade made to change a feeling is a trade made for the wrong reason.
- [ ] 20. **The regret test:** if this goes badly, will I be able to say "I followed my process and the outcome was bad" (acceptable) — or "I knew better and did it anyway" (not acceptable)?

---

## Decision

- [ ] All 20 answered in writing, dated, and saved.
- [ ] Any 72-hour or 30-day waiting period has fully elapsed.
- [ ] Trade executed as planned: ______________________
- [ ] Logged in [portfolio-review-template.md](portfolio-review-template.md) "Decisions" section with a review date set.

**If any box is unchecked, do nothing.** Doing nothing is a complete, respectable investing decision — it's the one your IPS makes by default.

## A note on selling

Selling has its own traps. Legitimate reasons to sell a long-term holding are few:

1. The thesis is broken (your "proves me wrong" facts occurred).
2. The position has grown past your IPS size limit.
3. Rebalancing per your rules.
4. Tax-loss harvesting (with an immediate swap into a similar holding).
5. You need the money for the goal it was saved for.
6. You found something clearly better *and* the switching cost (taxes, spread) is small.

"It's down a lot," "it's up a lot," "the market feels toppy," and "I'm nervous" are not on the list.
