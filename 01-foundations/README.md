# 01 — Foundations

Every investing strategy in this library — value, growth, indexing, dividends, factor tilts, options, real estate — rests on the same small set of facts: how compounding works, what stocks and bonds actually are, how much they have historically returned and how badly they have fallen, how a trade gets from your screen to a settled position, and which account wrappers let money grow with the least tax friction. Skip these and every later technique becomes a recipe followed without understanding the ingredients.

This section is written for a smart reader with no finance background. Each file stands alone, starts with a one-sentence summary, defines jargon as it goes, and ends with key takeaways and the primary sources behind every number. Read them in order the first time; afterward, use the glossary as a reference. The historical figures come mainly from Aswath Damodaran's NYU dataset (1928–2025) and the 2026 tax-year limits from the IRS.

- [asset-classes-explained.md](asset-classes-explained.md) — Stocks, bonds, cash, real estate/REITs, commodities, gold, crypto, and private markets: historical returns, volatility, correlations, and what each one does in a portfolio.
- [risk-and-return.md](risk-and-return.md) — Volatility, standard deviation, drawdowns, Sharpe ratio, beta, sequence-of-returns risk, risk tolerance vs. capacity vs. need, and a table of the worst U.S. crashes and how long recovery took.
- [how-markets-work.md](how-markets-work.md) — Exchanges, market makers, bid/ask spreads, order types, T+1 settlement, what an index is, and how individual stocks, mutual funds, and ETFs differ in cost and tax treatment.
- [investment-account-types.md](investment-account-types.md) — Taxable brokerage, 401(k), traditional and Roth IRA, HSA, 529, SEP and Solo 401(k), with the 2026 contribution limits and a sensible funding order.
- [glossary.md](glossary.md) — About 115 essential terms, alphabetical, one plain-language definition each.
