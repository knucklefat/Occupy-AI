# Podcasts and Video

**In one sentence:** Thirty-three podcasts and YouTube channels that teach rather than shout, grouped by what you will learn, with the episode to start on and who each show is for — all verified live in September 2026.

Audio is the best way to absorb how good investors think, because in a two-hour conversation people say what they would never write. It is also the worst way to get investing advice, because a confident voice is persuasive whether or not it is right. So this list leans toward interview shows with practitioners and academics, and away from anyone who tells you what to buy this week. All are free unless noted; every podcast is on Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and YouTube, so links go to the show's home page.

## Interview shows: how great investors think

| Show | Host | Start with | Why, and who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Invest Like the Best](https://colossus.com/series/invest-like-the-best/) | Patrick O'Shaughnessy | Episodes with Nick Sleep, Josh Wolfe, Bill Gurley, or Charlie Songhurst | The flagship investing interview show. Long, well-prepared conversations with fund managers, founders, and thinkers. Skews toward growth, venture, and technology. |
| [Business Breakdowns](https://colossus.com/series/business-breakdowns/) | Colossus (various hosts) | Costco, Visa, LVMH, or any company you own | One company per episode: how it makes money, its moat, its risks, with an investor who knows it well. The fastest way to learn how to analyze a business. |
| [Acquired](https://www.acquired.fm/) | Ben Gilbert & David Rosenthal | Berkshire Hathaway (parts 1–3), Costco, Nvidia, TSMC | Three-to-four-hour company histories, meticulously researched. More narrative than financial, but you finish understanding why the business exists. |
| [Founders](https://www.founderspodcast.com/) | David Senra | Episodes on Buffett, Munger, Rockefeller, or Sam Walton | Senra reads a biography a week and extracts the lessons. Not an investing show, but the best free education on what great business builders have in common. |
| [Capital Allocators](https://capitalallocators.com/) | Ted Seides | Episodes with endowment CIOs, Howard Marks, or the "Manager Meetings" series | How institutional money (endowments, pensions, family offices) picks managers and allocates. For anyone curious how the professional side actually works. |
| [Value Investing with Legends](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/value-investing-with-legends/id1459993408) | Columbia Business School (Heilbrunn Center) | Episodes with Mario Gabelli, Tom Russo, Leon Cooperman, Li Lu | Columbia's value program interviews the elders of the discipline. Twice-monthly, 77 episodes since 2019. |
| [We Study Billionaires (The Investor's Podcast)](https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/) | Stig Brodersen, Clay Finck, Kyle Grieve | The annual Berkshire meeting episodes and the intrinsic-value deep dives | The largest stock-investing podcast, Buffett-focused. Quality varies by host; the "Richer, Wiser, Happier" series with William Green is consistently good. |
| [Excess Returns](https://www.excessreturns.co/) | Jack Forehand, Matt Zeigler, and team | Episodes with Cliff Asness, Dan Rasmussen, or Meb Faber | Interviews with quant, value, and macro practitioners about process and evidence. Thoughtful hosts who ask good follow-up questions. |
| [Infinite Loops](https://www.infiniteloopspodcast.com/) | Jim O'Shaughnessy | Episodes with Morgan Housel or Annie Duke | Wide-ranging conversations about decision-making, markets, and human nature, from the author of *What Works on Wall Street*. |
| [Flirting with Models](https://www.flirtingwithmodels.com/) | Corey Hoffstein (Newfound Research) | Any episode on trend-following or return stacking | Quantitative investors explain how they research and build strategies. Technical; for people who already know what a factor is. |
| [Top Traders Unplugged](https://www.toptradersunplugged.com/) | Niels Kaastrup-Larsen | The "Systematic Investor" series | Trend-following, managed futures, and global macro from practitioners. Weekly, long-running, and the go-to for systematic trading. |

## Weekly markets and personal finance

| Show | Host | Start with | Why, and who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Animal Spirits](https://podcasts.thecompoundnews.com/show/animalspirits/) | Michael Batnick & Ben Carlson | Any recent episode | Two friends talk through the week's markets, listener questions, and charts. Casual, sane, and the best "what is going on" show for a non-professional. |
| [The Compound and Friends](https://podcasts.thecompoundnews.com/show/TCAF/) | Josh Brown, Michael Batnick, and guests | Episodes with a strategist or fund manager guest | Longer and livelier than Animal Spirits, with a rotating guest. Good for hearing how people on Wall Street are actually thinking. |
| [Rational Reminder](https://rationalreminder.ca/) | Ben Felix & Cameron Passmore (PWL Capital) | Episodes with Eugene Fama, Ken French, or John Cochrane; the "factor investing" episodes | The most rigorous evidence-based investing podcast. Canadian hosts, global relevance, academic guests. If you liked Bernstein's *Four Pillars*, this is the podcast version. |
| [Bogleheads on Investing](https://boglecenter.net/podcasts/) | Rick Ferri | Episodes with Bill Bernstein, Jack Bogle (2018), or Christine Benz | Monthly interviews on index investing, asset allocation, and retirement, sponsored by the Bogle Center. Unhurried and free of hype. |
| [The Long View](https://www.morningstar.com/podcasts/the-long-view) | Christine Benz & Dan Lefkovitz (Morningstar) | Episodes with Wade Pfau, Bill Bengen, or Jonathan Clements | Morningstar's weekly interview show on retirement, asset allocation, and personal finance. Especially strong on retirement withdrawal and Social Security. |
| [Masters in Business](https://ritholtz.com/about-masters-in-business/) | Barry Ritholtz (Bloomberg) | Episodes with Howard Marks, Ray Dalio, or Ken French | Ten years of long interviews with investors, economists, and authors. Transcripts are posted on The Big Picture. |
| [Money For the Rest of Us](https://moneyfortherestofus.com/) | David Stein | Any episode on bonds, REITs, or "should I invest in X" | A former institutional investment strategist explains one topic per episode in 30 minutes. Clear, structured, and good for intermediates. Paid "Plus" tier exists; the free show is complete on its own. |
| [Sound Investing](https://www.paulmerriman.com/podcasts) | Paul Merriman | The "Ultimate Buy and Hold" and small-cap value episodes | Evidence-based portfolio construction from a retired advisor turned educator, with free portfolio tables and data. Weekly since 2008. |
| [The Meb Faber Show](https://mebfaber.com/) | Meb Faber (Cambria) | Episodes with Ed Thorp or Rob Arnott | Global asset allocation, trend-following, and factor investing, plus interviews. Faber is opinionated but shows his data. |
| [Afford Anything](https://affordanything.com/podcast/) | Paula Pant | Any "First Friday" or Q&A episode | Personal finance and financial independence with a wider lens than most FIRE shows. Good for people in their 20s to 40s working out priorities. |
| [ChooseFI](https://www.choosefi.com/podcast/) | Brad Barrett | The early episodes on the "pillars of FI" | The financial independence community's main podcast, 750+ episodes. Practical, sometimes repetitive; best consumed selectively. |
| [Risk Parity Radio](https://www.riskparityradio.com/) | Frank Vasquez | Any episode explaining the sample portfolios | A retired lawyer's short, blunt show on diversified withdrawal-phase portfolios (risk parity, gold, small-cap value). Niche but useful for people near retirement. |

## Macro and markets

| Show | Host | Start with | Why, and who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Odd Lots](https://www.bloomberg.com/oddlots) | Joe Weisenthal & Tracy Alloway (Bloomberg) | Episodes on shipping, the repo market, or whatever asset is in the news | The best show for understanding how a specific market actually works, from container shipping to Treasury auctions. Curious, non-ideological, twice a week. |
| [MacroVoices](https://www.macrovoices.com/) | Erik Townsend & Patrick Ceresna | Any episode with Lyn Alden, Jim Bianco, or Luke Gromen | Weekly hour-long macro interviews, always free, with slide decks posted. Hosts are traders and it shows; more useful for framework than for trades. |
| [The Grant Williams Podcast](https://www.grant-williams.com/) | Grant Williams | Free episodes of "The End Game" | Long conversations with macro thinkers, often skeptical of central banks and bullish on gold. Most content is paid (tiered subscription); a few episodes are free. Listed for the quality of guests, with the caveat that the house view is persistently bearish. |
| [Real Vision](https://www.realvision.com/) | Raoul Pal and team | The free tier's interviews | Video-first macro and crypto interviews. Free tier plus paid tiers. Once excellent and broad; now heavily crypto-oriented. Use for interviews with specific guests, not for the house view. |

## YouTube channels

| Channel | Who | Start with | Why, and who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Aswath Damodaran](https://www.youtube.com/@AswathDamodaranonValuation) | NYU Stern professor | The 25-session "Valuation" playlist | His entire MBA valuation and corporate finance courses, free, updated every semester. The most valuable channel on this list by a wide margin. |
| [Ben Felix](https://www.youtube.com/@BenFelixCSI) | PWL Capital CIO | "Five Factor Investing with ETFs," "Do Stocks Return 10%?" | Ten-minute, footnoted videos summarizing the academic evidence on investing questions. If you want the research without reading the papers, start here. |
| [The Plain Bagel](https://www.youtube.com/@ThePlainBagel) | Richard Coffin, CFA, CFP | Any "explained" video | Calm, accurate explainers on investing basics, market events, and financial scams. Good for beginners and for correcting misinformation from other channels. |
| [Patrick Boyle](https://www.youtube.com/@PBoyle) | Former hedge fund manager, finance professor | Videos on any recent financial blow-up | Dry, funny post-mortems of frauds, bubbles, and bad trades, with real finance content underneath. |
| [Two Cents](https://www.youtube.com/@TwoCentsPBS) | PBS Digital Studios | Any episode | Short, well-produced personal finance basics. Good for teenagers and beginners. |
| [Rob Berger](https://www.youtube.com/@rob_berger) | Retired securities lawyer, author | Videos on retirement withdrawal strategies or the "3-fund portfolio" | Practical retirement and index investing walkthroughs, often using free tools live on screen. For DIY investors in their 40s to 60s. |
| [The Joseph Carlson Show](https://www.youtube.com/@JosephCarlsonShow) | Joseph Carlson | Any portfolio update | A retail investor documents his real dividend-growth portfolio over years. Entertaining and transparent; treat it as one person's journey, not a model, and note the quality-growth tilt. |
| [The Swedish Investor](https://www.youtube.com/@TheSwedishInvestor) | Anonymous Swedish investor | The animated summaries of *The Intelligent Investor* or *One Up on Wall Street* | Animated book summaries of the investing canon. Useful for deciding which book to read next. |

## Listening plan

- **New to investing:** The Plain Bagel and Two Cents on YouTube; Animal Spirits weekly; the first ten Bogleheads on Investing episodes.
- **Building a portfolio:** Rational Reminder (start with the factor episodes), Ben Felix's channel, The Long View.
- **Picking stocks:** Damodaran's valuation playlist first (about 8 hours), then Business Breakdowns and Value Investing with Legends.
- **Understanding markets:** Odd Lots and Money Stuff (the newsletter) together cover most of what happens.
- **Approaching retirement:** The Long View, Bogleheads on Investing, Risk Parity Radio, and the Early Retirement Now blog.

## Key takeaways

- Prefer interview shows with practitioners and academics (Invest Like the Best, Rational Reminder, Capital Allocators, Masters in Business) over shows with a house view.
- Damodaran's YouTube channel is a complete, free MBA valuation course; nothing else on this page is as valuable per hour.
- Business Breakdowns and Acquired teach how to analyze a company; Animal Spirits and Odd Lots teach what is happening in markets.
- Treat macro shows (MacroVoices, Grant Williams, Real Vision) as sources of frameworks and guests, not forecasts; their track records on timing are poor.
- Any podcast that makes you want to trade after listening is entertainment, not education.
