# 06 — Legendary Investors

The people in this section have beaten the market for decades, or in Bogle's case proved that most people shouldn't try. Each file covers who they are, a track record with numbers and years (and a flag wherever a number could not be verified from a primary or reliable source), the core of their philosophy, a set of rules a regular investor can actually use, the best things they've written, and the honest criticisms. The point is not hero worship. The point is that fifteen very different methods, from Buffett's decades-long holds to Simons' hours-long trades, converge on a small set of habits about costs, temperament, survival, and knowing your edge. Read the individual files for the methods; read [common-threads.md](common-threads.md) for the synthesis and a combined list of twenty principles. Start with Buffett, Graham, and Bogle if you're new; everything else builds on those three.

## A note on track records

Public, audited numbers exist for Berkshire Hathaway, Fidelity Magellan, Fundsmith, and the Vanguard 500. Private-fund numbers (Duquesne, Quantum, Baupost, Bridgewater, Gotham Capital, Medallion, Nomad, Pabrai, Aquamarine) are reported by the managers or reconstructed by journalists and are flagged as such in each file. Numbers that are widely repeated online but could not be traced to a reliable source are called out explicitly rather than quietly included.
