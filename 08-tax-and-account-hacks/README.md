# 08 — Tax and Account Hacks

Most investing advice obsesses over *what* to buy. This section is about the quieter lever that often matters more: **where you hold it, how you get money in and out, and what you pay along the way.** Taxes and fees are the two costs you can actually control, and the difference between a sloppy setup and a tight one compounds into six figures over a working life. Nothing here is a loophole in the pejorative sense. Every technique is a rule Congress wrote on purpose (backdoor Roth, HSA triple tax break, 0% capital gains bracket, qualified charitable distributions) or a plain consumer choice (paying 0.03% instead of 1%). All numbers are for **US investors, tax year 2026**, and are date-stamped inside each file; limits change every year, so check the "Sources" links before acting. This is education, not personalized tax advice — talk to a CPA for anything involving real money and edge cases.

*Last reviewed: September 2026.*

## Priority order of tax-advantaged accounts (2026)

```
START
  │
  ▼
[1] Emergency fund: 1–3 months of expenses in cash (grow to 3–6 later)
  │
  ▼
[2] 401(k)/403(b)/TSP up to the FULL employer match  ← free money, instant 50–100% return
  │
  ▼
[3] Kill high-interest debt (anything > ~7–8%: credit cards, personal loans)
  │
  ▼
[4] HSA to the max IF you have an HSA-eligible high-deductible plan
      2026: $4,400 self / $8,750 family (+$1,000 if 55+)   ← triple tax-free
  │
  ▼
[5] IRA to the max — Roth or Traditional (see roth-vs-traditional-decision.md)
      2026: $7,500 (+$1,100 if 50+). Over the income limit? → backdoor Roth.
  │
  ▼
[6] 401(k) to the employee max
      2026: $24,500 (+$8,000 if 50+; +$11,250 if age 60–63)
  │
  ▼
[7] Mega backdoor Roth IF your plan allows after-tax contributions
      2026 total plan limit: $72,000 (all sources)
  │
  ▼
[8] Pay down medium-interest debt (4–7%: car loans, many student loans) and/or
    fund a 529 if you have kids
  │
  ▼
[9] Taxable brokerage — tax-efficient index ETFs, held for the long term
  │
  ▼
[10] Extra mortgage payments / low-interest debt (optional, personal preference)
```
