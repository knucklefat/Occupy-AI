# Podcasts and Video

**In one sentence:** Crypto podcasting is where the industry talks to itself, so it is the fastest way to learn the arguments and the easiest place to get sold a token; the shows below are chosen for signal, with the sponsorship model and the tribe of each one spelled out.

## How to listen without getting sold something

Nearly every crypto podcast is funded by protocol, exchange or wallet sponsorships, and many hosts hold the tokens they discuss. The good ones disclose; the bad ones "just happen" to have a guest whose token is launching next week. Three habits help: notice who the sponsors are, notice whether the host ever has a critic on, and treat any episode with a founder as an infomercial with an interesting guest. The tribe label below (*Bitcoin*, *Ethereum/DeFi*, *Solana*, *Multi-chain*, *Skeptic*, *Mainstream*) tells you the default worldview.

All shows are free unless noted.

## Interview shows and news

**[Unchained](https://unchainedcrypto.com/)** — Laura Shin. *Multi-chain, journalist.* Twice weekly. The longest-running serious interview show; Shin is a former Forbes reporter, author of *The Cryptopians*, and one of the few hosts who asks hostile questions to guests she likes. Sponsored, but the sponsors do not pick the guests. Also on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@unchainedcrypto).

**[The Chopping Block](https://unchainedcrypto.com/podcasts/the-chopping-block/)** — Haseeb Qureshi, Tom Schmidt, Robert Leshner, Tarun Chitra. *Multi-chain, insiders.* Weekly. Four VCs and founders arguing about the week's news with unusual candour about what does not work. Every one of them has a book, and they say so; that transparency is the appeal.

**[Bankless](https://www.bankless.com/podcast)** — Ryan Sean Adams and David Hoffman. *Ethereum/DeFi.* Several times weekly. The largest Ethereum-native show, high energy, explicitly bullish. Excellent for understanding what the Ethereum community believes and why; weak on critics, and heavily sponsored. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Bankless).

**[The Breakdown](https://nlw.substack.com/)** — Nathaniel Whittemore. *Multi-chain, macro.* Daily, short. A ten-to-twenty-minute, even-handed summary of what the industry is discussing, with more attention to policy and macro than most. NLW now spends most of his time on AI, and the crypto show is lighter than in 2021–23. Also on [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-breakdown/id1438693620).

**[Empire](https://www.blockworks.co/podcast/empire)** — Jason Yanowitz and Santiago Santos. *Multi-chain, institutional.* Twice weekly. Blockworks' flagship; founder and investor interviews with a business-model focus. Sponsored by protocols; the hosts are investors. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Empirepod).

**[0xResearch](https://www.blockworks.co/podcast/0xresearch)** — Blockworks Research team. *Multi-chain, analytical.* Weekly. Analysts discussing tokenomics, revenue and on-chain data rather than narratives; a good complement to Empire.

**[Lightspeed](https://www.youtube.com/@LightspeedpodHQ)** — Blockworks. *Solana.* Several times weekly. The main Solana-ecosystem show; founders, validators and traders. Sponsored by Solana projects and unapologetically home-team; useful precisely because the Ethereum shows ignore Solana and vice versa.

**[Odd Lots](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/odd-lots/id1056200096)** — Joe Weisenthal and Tracy Alloway, Bloomberg. *Mainstream.* Several times weekly; crypto episodes are a minority. When Odd Lots does crypto (Tether, MicroStrategy, the ETF, stablecoin plumbing, the FTX collapse) it is the best explanation a non-crypto investor will hear, because the hosts do not need crypto to succeed. Free; also on the [Bloomberg site](https://www.bloomberg.com/podcasts/series/odd-lots).

**[Coin Bureau](https://www.coinbureau.com/)** — Guy Turner. *Multi-chain, retail.* Weekly YouTube videos plus a podcast. The largest retail-facing crypto channel, with polished explainers. **Caveat:** the business model is affiliate exchange links and a paid membership, the coverage extends to small-cap tokens, and the "review" format blurs education and promotion. Fine for learning what a thing is; not a source of investment ideas. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@CoinBureau).

**[Into the Cryptoverse](https://intothecryptoverse.com/)** — Benjamin Cowen. *Multi-chain, technical analysis.* Near-daily YouTube. A former engineer who does "lengthening cycles," risk-metric and dominance-chart analysis. **Caveat:** the analysis is chart-fitting to a very short history, is delivered with more confidence than the sample size supports, and sells a premium subscription. Cowen is more measured than most TA channels and will say "bearish"; still, treat cycle predictions as entertainment.

**[Zero Knowledge](https://www.zeroknowledge.fm/)** — Anna Rose. *Ethereum/cryptography.* Weekly. Deep interviews with cryptographers and protocol researchers on zero-knowledge proofs, rollups and consensus. For the technically curious; sponsored by ZK projects.

**[Molly White's Citation Needed](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/molly-whites-citation-needed/id1719025552)** — *Skeptic.* Audio versions of the newsletter issues. Documented, calm and relentless.

**[Real Vision](https://www.youtube.com/@RealVisionFinance)** — Raoul Pal and guests. *Multi-chain, macro-bull.* Daily. Pal was an early institutional Bitcoin and Ethereum advocate and coined "the banana zone." **Caveat:** perma-bullish, sells a paid tier, and has promoted small-cap tokens; the macro interviews with non-crypto guests are the valuable part.

## Bitcoin-only

**[Bitcoin Fundamentals (The Investor's Podcast Network)](https://www.theinvestorspodcast.com/bitcoin-fundamentals/)** — Preston Pysh. *Bitcoin.* Weekly. A value investor who read Buffett for a decade and then went all in on Bitcoin; the show interviews Bitcoin's investors and thinkers with a traditional-finance vocabulary. Bullish, but the host asks about valuation, which is rare.

**[Stephan Livera Podcast](https://stephanlivera.com/)** — *Bitcoin, Austrian economics.* Twice weekly. Long, substantive interviews on Bitcoin economics, self-custody and privacy; Livera is a chartered accountant and works at a Bitcoin custody firm. The right show for the technical and economic case, made by a maximalist who is courteous to critics.

**[TFTC (Tales from the Crypt)](https://tftc.io/)** — Marty Bent. *Bitcoin, maximalist.* Several times weekly. Interviews with miners, developers and Bitcoin-industry founders; Bent runs a mining company and the show reflects it. The weekly **[Rabbit Hole Recap](https://rhr.tv/)** with Matt Odell is the Bitcoin-community news roundup, opinionated and with a strong self-custody and privacy focus.

**[Mr Obnoxious / The Peter McCormack Show](https://www.petermccormack.com/)** — Peter McCormack. *Bitcoin, broadening.* McCormack's *What Bitcoin Did* was for years the biggest Bitcoin interview show, notable for hosting critics (he interviewed Nouriel Roubini and skeptics of all kinds). He ended it in 2024 and now hosts a broader show on money, politics and power; the *What Bitcoin Did* back catalogue remains at [whatbitcoindid.com](https://www.whatbitcoindid.com/) and is the best archive of the 2018–2024 Bitcoin conversation.

**[Bitcoin Audible](https://bitcoinaudible.com/)** — Guy Swann. *Bitcoin.* Several times weekly. Swann reads the best Bitcoin essays aloud with commentary; a painless way to cover the canonical writing (Boyapati, Gigi, Alden, Carter) on a commute.

**[BTC Sessions](https://www.youtube.com/@BTCSessions)** — Ben Perrin. *Bitcoin, practical.* YouTube. Step-by-step tutorials on wallets, hardware devices, nodes and Lightning. No investment content; the best video source for actually learning to self-custody.

**[On the Brink with Castle Island](https://onthebrink-podcast.com/)** — Nic Carter and Matt Walsh. *Bitcoin and stablecoins, institutional.* Weekly-ish. Two VC partners on policy, banking access and stablecoin regulation; Carter's contrarian streak keeps it from being a cheerleading session.

## Ethereum and DeFi

**[The Daily Gwei](https://www.thedailygwei.com/)** — Anthony Sassano. *Ethereum.* Daily YouTube "Refuel." A daily, bullish, well-informed summary of Ethereum ecosystem news; Sassano discloses his ETH position. [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@TheDailyGwei).

**[Finematics](https://www.youtube.com/@Finematics)** — *DeFi, education.* Animated explainers of how AMMs, lending protocols, flash loans and rollups work. Irregular now, but the back catalogue is still the clearest visual introduction to DeFi mechanics.

**[aantonop](https://www.youtube.com/@aantonop)** — Andreas M. Antonopoulos. *Bitcoin and Ethereum, education.* The *Mastering Bitcoin* author's talks and Q&A archive. His 2014–2019 talks ("The Internet of Money") are still the best plain-language explanations of why the technology matters; he holds both BTC and ETH and has been attacked by both tribes for it.

## Skeptics

**[Crypto Critics' Corner](https://cryptocriticscorner.com/)** — Cas Piancey and Bennett Tomlin. *Skeptic.* Weekly-ish. The main long-form critical podcast: Tether, exchange solvency, influencer conflicts, court cases, with guests who include prosecutors and short-sellers. Piancey is a journalist and Tomlin a data scientist; both are polite, thorough and have been right about a lot. Also on [Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/crypto-critics-corner/id1557045965).

**[Odd Lots](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/odd-lots/id1056200096)** and **[Citation Needed](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/molly-whites-citation-needed/id1719025552)** (above) are the other two shows that will reliably present the case against.

## A starting rotation

| Time per week | Listen to |
|---|---|
| One hour | Unchained (one episode) |
| Two to three hours | Add The Chopping Block and one Odd Lots crypto episode |
| Five hours | Add Bitcoin Fundamentals or Stephan Livera (Bitcoin), Bankless or The Daily Gwei (Ethereum), and Crypto Critics' Corner |
| Daily commute | The Breakdown for headlines, Bitcoin Audible for essays |

## Key takeaways

- The best interview shows (Unchained, The Chopping Block, Odd Lots) are the ones where the host has a reason to be skeptical of the guest; prefer journalists and generalists over founders interviewing founders.
- Each tribe has its own shows and ignores the others; listen to one Bitcoin show, one Ethereum show and one Solana show to hear what each side will not tell you.
- Coin Bureau, Benjamin Cowen and Real Vision are useful for vocabulary and terrible for timing; their business models depend on you staying engaged, not on being right.
- Crypto Critics' Corner and Citation Needed are the required skeptic listening; if you cannot answer their objections, you do not understand your position.
- *What Bitcoin Did* is over and *Week in Ethereum News* is over; archives outlive shows, and a good archive (WBD, aantonop, Finematics) is often better than a live feed.
