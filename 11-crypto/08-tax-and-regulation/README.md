# Crypto Tax and Regulation (US)

**In one sentence:** The IRS treats crypto as property, so nearly every trade, swap, spend, and reward is a reportable event, and since 2025 the exchanges are sending the IRS your numbers directly, while Washington has spent 2025–26 replacing enforcement-by-lawsuit with actual rules.

This sub-section covers what a US investor needs to know as of **September 11, 2026**: how gains and income are taxed, which forms to file, what the new Form 1099-DA changes, how to plan around losses, gifts, retirement accounts, and residency, and where the regulatory ground has shifted (GENIUS Act, SEC–CFTC taxonomy, spot ETF listing standards, the pending CLARITY Act). Every file date-stamps its claims and flags what could not be confirmed. Nothing here is individual tax advice; the rules cited change often, and the pending wash-sale and market-structure legislation could alter several of them within months. Use these pages to ask better questions of a crypto-literate CPA, and to avoid the expensive, common mistakes: unreported swaps, zero-basis 1099-DA matching, and sloppy lot tracking.

## What triggers a taxable event

| You did this | Taxable? | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Bought crypto with USD | No | — (record basis and date) |
| Held through a 5x run | No | — |
| Moved coins between your own wallets | No (the tiny gas fee paid in crypto technically is) | — |
| Sold crypto for USD | **Yes** | Capital gain/loss |
| Swapped one coin for another (incl. to a stablecoin) | **Yes** | Capital gain/loss |
| Spent crypto on goods or services | **Yes** | Capital gain/loss |
| Received staking, mining, or interest rewards | **Yes** | Ordinary income at receipt |
| Received an airdrop you can access | **Yes** | Ordinary income at receipt |
| Got paid in crypto | **Yes** | Wages or self-employment income |
| Entered or exited a liquidity pool | **Probably yes** (conservative view: a swap) | Capital gain/loss |
| Wrapped or bridged a token | **Gray area** | Depends on position taken |
| Received a gift of crypto | No (donor may file Form 709 above $19,000) | — |
| Donated appreciated crypto to charity | No gain; possible deduction | — |
| Sold an NFT that is a "collectible" | **Yes** | Capital gain, up to 28% long-term rate |

## Key dates to watch (as of September 2026)

- **September 15, 2026:** Senate cloture vote on the CLARITY Act (market structure).
- **~October 18, 2026:** comment deadline on the SEC's proposed Regulation Crypto Assets.
- **January 2027:** Form 1099-DA for 2026 activity arrives with **cost basis** for the first time.
- **By January 18, 2027:** GENIUS Act stablecoin rules take effect (earlier if final rules are published sooner).
- **Pending, no date:** wash-sale extension, $300 de minimis exemption, staking-income deferral (Lummis bill; PARITY Act).
