# Crypto Techniques: Buying, Holding, Securing, and Managing

This sub-section is the mechanics. The foundations and strategy files explain *why* someone might own bitcoin, ether, or stablecoins; these files explain *how* to do it without paying 2% on every purchase, losing coins to a phone call, or leaving heirs a locked box. Crypto differs from stocks in three practical ways that drive everything here: transactions are irreversible, custody is your problem unless you deliberately outsource it, and no single institution sees your full history for tax purposes. Each file follows the same pattern — plain-language explanation, a comparison table where numbers matter, a step-by-step "how to actually do it," takeaways, and sources. Figures (fees, loss statistics, network costs) are date-stamped as of September 2026 and will drift; the procedures should not. Read them in order if you are starting from zero, or jump to the one that matches the problem in front of you.
