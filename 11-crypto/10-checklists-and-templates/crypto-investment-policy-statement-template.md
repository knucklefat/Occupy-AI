# Crypto Investment Policy Statement (IPS) Template

**In one sentence:** A fill-in-the-blank policy that fixes, in advance and in writing, how much crypto you may own, how you will hold it, when you will buy and sell, what you will never do, how you will handle taxes, and when you will review — so that decisions get made by the calm version of you rather than the one watching a 40% candle.

**How this relates to your main IPS:** This is a *supplement* to the general investment policy statement in the main library ([../../10-checklists-and-templates/investment-policy-statement-template.md](../../10-checklists-and-templates/investment-policy-statement-template.md)). Your overall asset allocation lives there; this document governs the crypto sleeve inside it. If the two ever conflict, the main IPS wins.

**How to use it:** Copy the blank template, fill every blank, sign and date it, and store it with your other financial documents (and a copy where you will see it during a crash). Change it only at a scheduled review, never on a day the market moved more than 10%.

## Blank template

### 1. Purpose and role of crypto in my portfolio

Crypto's role in my portfolio is: ______________________________________
(e.g., "a small, high-volatility diversifier with asymmetric upside," "a long-term bet on bitcoin as a monetary asset," "speculation with money I can afford to lose")

Time horizon for this sleeve: ______ years. I will not judge the decision on anything shorter than ______ years.

### 2. Allocation limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| **Maximum crypto allocation** (% of total investable assets, measured at market value) | ______% |
| **Target crypto allocation** | ______% |
| **Minimum allocation** (below which I rebalance up, if any) | ______% |
| Maximum in **bitcoin** (% of crypto sleeve) | ______% |
| Maximum in **ether** (% of crypto sleeve) | ______% |
| Maximum in **all other tokens combined** (% of crypto sleeve) | ______% |
| Maximum in **any single non-BTC/ETH token** (% of crypto sleeve) | ______% |
| Maximum in **DeFi / yield positions** (% of crypto sleeve) | ______% |
| Maximum in **stablecoins held as cash** (% of crypto sleeve) | ______% |

Crypto counts toward my overall **risk-asset** bucket (stocks + crypto + other speculative). Combined, that bucket will not exceed ______% of investable assets.

### 3. Rebalancing rules

- I check the allocation on the ______ of every ______ (month / quarter).
- **Upper band:** if crypto exceeds ______% of investable assets (target × ______), I sell down to the target within ______ days.
- **Lower band:** if crypto falls below ______% (target × ______), I buy up to the target within ______ days — *only from cash already designated for it, never from emergency funds or by selling other assets I would not otherwise sell*.
- Within the sleeve, I rebalance between BTC / ETH / other when any sub-limit above is breached by more than ______ percentage points.
- I rebalance in the most tax-efficient account first: ______________________ (e.g., "sell in the IRA-held ETF before selling self-custodied coins").

### 4. Buying rules

- **Method:** dollar-cost averaging of $______ every ______ (week / month), on the ______ day, via ______________________ (exchange / brokerage).
- I do not make discretionary buys larger than $______ without completing [pre-trade-checklist-for-crypto.md](pre-trade-checklist-for-crypto.md) and waiting ______ hours.
- I do not buy any token outside BTC/ETH unless it scores at least ______ on [token-due-diligence-checklist.md](token-due-diligence-checklist.md).
- I do not buy anything I first heard about from a DM, an influencer, a group chat, or an ad.

### 5. Selling and take-profit rules

- **Systematic:** rebalancing (section 3) is my primary selling mechanism.
- **Take-profit ladder for non-BTC/ETH tokens:** sell ______% at ______× cost, ______% at ______× cost, and ______% at ______× cost. Recover initial cost by ______× at the latest.
- **Thesis-break rule:** if any of the written "what would prove me wrong" conditions occurs, I sell within ______ days regardless of price.
- **Stop rule for speculative positions:** if a position falls ______% below cost and the thesis is not clearly intact, I exit.
- I never sell BTC/ETH purely because of price movement; I sell them only via rebalancing, for planned spending, or for tax-loss harvesting with immediate repurchase (see section 8).

### 6. Custody rules

- Any exchange balance above $______ (or older than ______ days) is withdrawn to self-custody.
- Long-term holdings are stored in: ______________________ (hardware wallet single-sig / passphrase / 2-of-3 multisig / collaborative custody / ETF in brokerage).
- Seed backups: ______ copies, on ______ (paper / metal), at locations: ______________________ and ______________________. Recovery last tested on ______.
- DeFi and experimental activity happens **only** from a separate wallet holding no more than $______.
- Heirs' access letter is stored at ______________________ and was last updated ______.

### 7. Approved venues

| Venue | Approved for | Max balance |
|---|---|---|
| ______________ (exchange) | buying, selling, short-term holding | $______ |
| ______________ (brokerage / ETF) | ETF exposure in tax-advantaged accounts | no limit within allocation |
| ______________ (DeFi protocol) | ______ | $______ |

Adding a venue requires completing [exchange-selection-checklist.md](exchange-selection-checklist.md) or [defi-protocol-safety-checklist.md](defi-protocol-safety-checklist.md) and a ______-day waiting period.

### 8. Forbidden actions (no exceptions without a rewritten IPS at a scheduled review)

- [ ] I will not use **leverage, margin, or perpetual futures** in any form.
- [ ] I will not deposit into **unaudited or sub-6-month-old yield products**, or any product whose yield source I cannot explain in one sentence.
- [ ] I will not **lend coins to a centralized platform** for yield.
- [ ] I will not **sign a transaction or message I do not understand**, or grant unlimited token approvals.
- [ ] I will not connect my vault wallet to any website.
- [ ] I will not buy on the day of a 20%+ move in either direction.
- [ ] I will not share my seed phrase, passphrase, or holdings with anyone, ever, for any reason.
- [ ] I will not change this document in response to a price move.
- [ ] Other: ______________________________________

### 9. Tax rules

- I record every transaction in ______________________ (tool) within ______ days.
- When I realize a gain, I move ______% of the gain into the "crypto tax set-aside" account the same week (rule of thumb: ~30% for short-term gains, ~20–25% for long-term; adjust for your bracket and state).
- I will harvest losses when a position is down more than ______% or $______, following [tax-record-keeping-checklist.md](tax-record-keeping-checklist.md).
- I will prefer to hold appreciated positions past ______ months for long-term treatment unless a thesis-break or rebalance rule requires otherwise.

### 10. Crash protocol

When the crypto sleeve falls more than ______% from its high, I will follow [crypto-crash-playbook.md](crypto-crash-playbook.md): verify custody, wait ______ hours, re-read this document, then act only on the rebalancing and tax-harvest rules above.

### 11. Review schedule

- **Quarterly:** allocation vs. bands, exchange sweep, approval revocation, transaction export.
- **Annually (on ______):** re-read and re-affirm this IPS; test wallet recovery; update firmware; update heirs' letter; review venue list.
- **Triggered review:** a change in income, a major life event, a venue failure, or a regulatory change affecting my holdings.

Signed: ______________________ Date: __________
Partner / accountability person (optional): ______________________

## Filled example: "Priya," 34, software engineer, $400k investable assets

> *Fictional investor for illustration. Numbers are chosen to be internally consistent, not to be recommendations.*

### 1. Purpose
Crypto is a small, high-volatility diversifier in my portfolio with a long-term thesis on bitcoin as a scarce digital monetary asset and ether as the settlement layer for tokenized finance. Time horizon: 10+ years. I will not judge the decision on anything shorter than 4 years (one full cycle).

### 2. Allocation limits
| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Maximum crypto allocation | 6% |
| Target | 4% |
| Minimum | 2% |
| Max bitcoin (of sleeve) | 100% |
| Max ether (of sleeve) | 40% |
| Max all other tokens | 15% |
| Max single other token | 5% |
| Max DeFi / yield | 10% |
| Max stablecoins as cash | 10% |

Risk-asset bucket (stocks + crypto): max 85% of investable assets.

### 3. Rebalancing
Checked on the 1st of each quarter. Upper band 6% → sell to 4% within 10 days. Lower band 2% → buy to 4% within 10 days from the "crypto rebalancing cash" sub-account only. Sub-limits rebalanced when breached by more than 5 points. Rebalance in the Roth IRA-held bitcoin ETF first, self-custodied coins last.

### 4. Buying
$300 every Friday split 70/30 BTC/ETH on Kraken. No discretionary buy above $1,000 without the pre-trade checklist and a 48-hour wait. Non-BTC/ETH tokens require a due-diligence score of 34+.

### 5. Selling
Rebalancing is the main mechanism. Altcoin ladder: 25% at 2×, 25% at 4×, 25% at 8×; initial cost recovered by 4× at the latest. Thesis-break → sell within 7 days. Speculative stop: −60% from cost with unclear thesis → exit. BTC/ETH sold only via rebalancing or tax-loss harvesting.

### 6. Custody
Exchange balance above $2,000 or older than 30 days → withdrawn. Long-term holdings on a hardware wallet with passphrase; will move to 2-of-3 multisig when the sleeve exceeds $50,000. Seed: two metal copies, home safe and parents' safe-deposit box; passphrase stored separately at each. Recovery tested 2026-03-14. DeFi wallet capped at $1,500. Heirs' letter with the will at the attorney's office, updated 2026-03.

### 7. Approved venues
| Venue | Approved for | Max balance |
|---|---|---|
| Kraken | buying, selling, short-term holding | $2,000 |
| Fidelity Roth IRA (spot bitcoin ETF) | tax-advantaged BTC exposure | within allocation |
| Aave v3 (Ethereum mainnet) | lending stablecoins from DeFi wallet | $1,500 |

### 8. Forbidden actions
All nine listed, plus: "I will not buy any token with a fully diluted valuation below $100M."

### 9. Tax
Koinly, updated monthly. 30% of realized gains → high-yield savings "crypto tax" account the same week. Harvest losses when a lot is down more than 25%. Prefer holding past 12 months.

### 10. Crash
At −40% from the sleeve's high: verify custody, wait 48 hours, re-read this, then rebalance and harvest per rules. Nothing else.

### 11. Review
Quarterly on the 1st; annual on my birthday. Signed 2026-04-02; accountability partner: my spouse.
