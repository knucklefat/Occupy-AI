# Pre-Trade Checklist for Crypto

**In one sentence:** The twelve things to write down in the ten minutes before any non-automated crypto buy — thesis, category, valuation, unlocks, liquidity, venue, fees, custody, tax lot, emotional state, size, and exit — so the trade is a decision rather than a reflex.

**When to use it:** Every discretionary purchase (anything that is not your scheduled DCA). Especially when you feel urgency. The checklist takes ten minutes; the median crypto mistake is made in ten seconds.

**Rule:** If you cannot fill in every line, you do not buy today. The trade will still be there tomorrow, and if it will not, that tells you something about the trade.

---

## The checklist

**Date:** __________ **Asset:** __________ **Proposed amount:** $__________

### 1. Thesis (one sentence)
- [ ] I can state why this asset should be worth more in ______ years in a single sentence a non-crypto friend would understand:
  "_________________________________________________________________"
- [ ] I can state what would prove the thesis wrong: "_________________________________"
- [ ] The thesis is about the asset, not about the price ("it's going up" is not a thesis).

### 2. Category and role
- [ ] Category: ☐ BTC ☐ ETH ☐ large-cap L1/L2 ☐ DeFi token ☐ stablecoin ☐ memecoin / narrative play ☐ other: ______
- [ ] Role in the sleeve: ☐ core long-term ☐ satellite ☐ speculative "lottery ticket"
- [ ] After this buy, the category sub-limit in my [IPS](crypto-investment-policy-statement-template.md) is still respected: current ______% → after ______% (limit ______%).

### 3. Valuation: FDV and supply
- [ ] Market cap (circulating): $__________ **FDV** (price × total supply): $__________
- [ ] Circulating / total supply ratio: ______%. If below ~30%, I understand most of the supply is still to come.
- [ ] For non-BTC/ETH tokens: I have compared FDV with revenue or usage and with 2–3 peers (see [token-due-diligence-checklist.md](token-due-diligence-checklist.md)); this token scored ______ / 48.

### 4. Unlocks and emissions
- [ ] Next major unlock: date ______, size ______% of circulating supply, recipient ______.
- [ ] Annual inflation from emissions: ______%.
- [ ] No unlock larger than ~5% of circulating supply lands within the next 60 days — or I have a reason to buy in front of it anyway: ______________________.

### 5. Liquidity
- [ ] 24h volume: $__________ across ______ venues. Volume / market cap ratio: ______.
- [ ] Estimated slippage (price impact) for my order size on my chosen venue: ______%.
- [ ] I could sell 5× my position within a day without moving the price more than ~2%. If not, this is speculative size only.

### 6. Where I am buying and why
- [ ] Venue: ______________________ (on my approved list: ☐ yes ☐ no → stop).
- [ ] For a DEX purchase: contract address verified against the project's official site *and* CoinGecko/CoinMarketCap: __________ ✓. Not a copycat ticker.
- [ ] I reached the venue from my own bookmark, not a link.

### 7. Fees and network
- [ ] Trading fee: ______% Spread vs. mid-market: ______% Network/gas fee: $______ Withdrawal fee: $______
- [ ] All-in round-trip cost (buy + sell + withdraw): ______%. The thesis needs to overcome this before I make anything.
- [ ] For a DEX trade: slippage tolerance set to ______% (not the default), and the wallet simulation shows exactly what leaves and arrives.

### 8. Custody plan
- [ ] Where this asset will live after purchase: ☐ exchange (≤ IPS cap, sweep by ______) ☐ hardware wallet ☐ multisig ☐ DeFi wallet ☐ ETF in brokerage
- [ ] The wallet/network supports this asset natively; I have done a test transfer of this asset before, or will send a small test first.
- [ ] Approvals granted for the purchase will be revoked afterwards: ☐ n/a ☐ scheduled.

### 9. Tax lot
- [ ] This buy creates a new lot: date ______, units ______, cost basis $______ (price × units + fees).
- [ ] If I am funding the buy by selling another crypto asset, that is a **taxable disposal** of the asset I am selling: estimated gain/loss $______, short/long-term ______, tax set-aside moved: ☐.
- [ ] Logged in my tax tool by: ______ (date).

### 10. "Am I FOMOing?" (answer honestly)
- [ ] Is the price up more than 20% in the last 7 days? ☐ yes ☐ no
- [ ] Did I first hear about this in the last 48 hours? ☐ yes ☐ no
- [ ] Did the idea come from a DM, group chat, influencer, or ad? ☐ yes ☐ no
- [ ] Do I feel that I have to buy *now* or miss it? ☐ yes ☐ no
- [ ] Am I trying to make back a recent loss? ☐ yes ☐ no
- [ ] Have I already told someone I'm buying it? ☐ yes ☐ no

**Two or more "yes" answers → wait 48 hours and redo the checklist.** Three or more → the answer is no.

### 11. Sizing check
- [ ] Amount as % of investable assets: ______%. As % of crypto sleeve: ______%.
- [ ] If this goes to zero, my total portfolio falls ______%. I can say "that's fine" out loud without flinching.
- [ ] Volatility check (see [crypto-formulas-and-rules-of-thumb.md](crypto-formulas-and-rules-of-thumb.md)): with this asset's annualized volatility of ~______%, this position contributes about the same risk as a ______% stock position. That is within my plan.
- [ ] Total crypto after this buy: ______% of investable assets — inside the IPS maximum of ______%.

### 12. Exit plan
- [ ] Take-profit: sell ______% at ______×, ______% at ______×.
- [ ] Thesis-break exit: within ______ days of any "proves me wrong" event above.
- [ ] Time-based review: I will re-evaluate on ______ (date) whatever the price.
- [ ] For speculative positions: I will exit if it falls ______% from cost with no clear thesis support.
- [ ] I have written these into my tracking sheet, not just in my head.

---

## Decision

☐ **Buy as planned** — every line filled, FOMO score ≤ 1, size and category within IPS.
☐ **Buy smaller** ($______) — liquidity, score, or FOMO answers argue for lottery-ticket size.
☐ **Wait 48 hours** — FOMO score ≥ 2, or missing information.
☐ **No** — any hard fail: not on approved venue, wrong contract, unlock imminent, above IPS limit, or I cannot state the thesis.
