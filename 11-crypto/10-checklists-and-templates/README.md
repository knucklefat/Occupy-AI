# Crypto Checklists and Templates

**In one sentence:** Ready-to-use checklists, fill-in templates, and formulas that turn everything else in the crypto section into safe, repeatable action.

Crypto punishes improvisation. The losses that actually end people's participation — a seed phrase typed into a phishing site, a balance stranded on an insolvent exchange, a token bought at the top of an unlock cliff, a leveraged "dip buy," a tax bill on gains that have since evaporated — are almost never failures of knowledge. They are failures of process at a moment of emotion. A checklist is a way of making the calm version of you the one who decides.

Every file here is grounded in published guidance from hardware-wallet makers, regulated exchanges, the FBI and FTC, the IRS, and on-chain analytics providers, rewritten in plain language with jargon defined inline. Checklists use tick boxes; templates are fill-in-the-blank with a worked example. Start with the 30-day plan if you are new, the IPS template if you already hold crypto, and the crash playbook before you need it.
