# 11 — Crypto

**In one sentence:** A complete sub-library on digital assets, built with the same structure and standards as the rest of Occupy AI — how the technology works, how people invest in it, how to hold it safely, how to analyze it, what to watch, who to read (bulls and skeptics alike), how it's taxed and regulated in the US, how it has blown up, and the checklists that keep you out of the wreckage.

**95 files · ~155,000 words · researched and fact-checked September 2026.**

> **Read this first.** Crypto is the most contested asset class in this library. Bitcoin has produced enormous returns since 2010 and has also fallen 77–93% in every cycle; the median retail crypto app user has lost money; and the industry's history includes some of the largest frauds in financial history. This section takes no side. Where evidence exists it is cited; where an idea is a belief, it is labeled as one. The library's overall position is in [Asset classes explained](../01-foundations/asset-classes-explained.md): a speculative satellite sized so an 80% loss doesn't change your life — for most people 0–5% — and only after the rest of the plan is in place.
