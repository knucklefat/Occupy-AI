# Risk Management Rules for Crypto

**In one sentence:** Every rule below exists because someone lost everything by not following it; the rules are boring on purpose, and the one-page version at the end is meant to be printed and signed.

## Why a rule set, not judgment

The evidence in this section is consistent: most retail crypto buyers lose money (BIS), the losses cluster in leverage, altcoins, exchanges and scams (Chainalysis, FBI), and the decisions that cause them are made under exactly the conditions (late night, falling price, social pressure) where judgment is worst. Rules written in a calm moment are the only defense that works when you are not calm. Treat this as the crypto annex to your [investment policy statement](../../10-checklists-and-templates/investment-policy-statement-template.md).

## The rules

### 1. Sizing

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Total crypto allocation: a percentage of net worth you could lose entirely without changing any life plan. For most people that is 1–5%; for the highly convinced with secure finances, perhaps up to 10%. | Every completed cycle has produced a 77–93% BTC drawdown; a 100% loss of altcoins is the base case. |
| Core (BTC, and if you choose, ETH): at least 80% of the crypto allocation. | The only assets that have recovered from every drawdown so far. |
| Speculative sleeve (everything else): at most 20% of the crypto allocation, so at most 1–2% of net worth. | Three cohorts of altcoins have gone to near zero (2018, 2022, 2026). |
| No single small token above a quarter of the speculative sleeve. | Rug pulls and insider launches are structural, not rare. |
| Rebalance to target on a calendar (quarterly or when it drifts by half its target). | The only mechanism that sells at tops and buys at bottoms without forecasting. |

### 2. Leverage: none

No margin, no perpetual futures, no borrowing against crypto, no "just 2x." The October 10, 2025 cascade liquidated 1.6 million accounts and $19 billion in one day, including traders whose collateral was mispriced by their own exchange. Leveraged retail traders are net losers in every dataset. If you want more exposure, buy more spot within your sizing rule. If you cannot resist, you have a gambling problem to address, not a trading strategy to refine.

### 3. Custody thresholds

| Balance | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| Anything you would be upset to lose | Self-custody hardware wallet with the seed phrase backed up offline in two separate physical locations; or a spot ETF / regulated qualified custodian if you accept counterparty risk for simplicity |
| More than roughly one month of living expenses on any exchange | Not allowed; withdraw |
| Active DeFi or NFT wallet | A separate "hot" wallet with only what you are using this week; it never holds core |
| Seed phrase | Never typed into a website, photographed, or stored in a cloud note; never given to "support" |

Test a full withdrawal from every exchange you use before you need it. Use hardware-key two-factor authentication, not SMS. Use a dedicated email for exchange accounts.

### 4. Exchange limits

- Use only large, regulated exchanges in your jurisdiction; check that they publish proof of reserves, and remember it proves assets on one day, not solvency.
- No more than 10–20% of total crypto on any single exchange at any time; a trading float only.
- Diversify stablecoin issuers for balances that matter; prefer issuers with monthly attested T-bill reserves. USDC traded at ~$0.87 during the March 2023 bank weekend; treat stablecoins as near-cash, not cash.
- Warning signs that preceded every failure in this section: withdrawal delays, changed terms of service, executive departures, a related-party token on the balance sheet, yields above the market. On any of them, withdraw first and investigate second.
- Assume no deposit insurance exists. FDIC and SIPC do not cover crypto.

### 5. Yield you do not understand: none

The source of yield must be explainable in one sentence that does not depend on new deposits: "validators are paid in new issuance and fees for securing the chain" is acceptable; "the platform's trading desk generates returns" is not. Every collapse that took customer yield deposits (BitConnect, Celsius, Voyager, Anchor/Terra, the USDe promotion before October 2025) failed this test. If the yield exceeds the risk-free rate by more than a few points, assume it is compensation for a risk of total loss and size it as such.

### 6. Take-profit rules

- When the crypto allocation exceeds 1.5x its target after a rally, sell back to target. Mechanically. That is your only reliable signal that you are near a top.
- When any speculative position is up 3x or more, sell at least the original stake. You now own a free position and cannot lose money on it.
- Convert realized gains into the boring assets in your plan (index funds, bonds, cash). "Recycling" gains into the next token is how bull-market profits become bear-market losses.
- Never raise the target allocation because the price rose. Rising price is evidence you are late, not early.

### 7. Scam defense

- Unsolicited contact about crypto, from anyone, is a scam until proven otherwise through a second channel you already had.
- Never sign a wallet transaction you did not initiate from a URL you typed yourself. No "verify," "claim," "recover" or "migrate" signatures.
- No celebrity is giving away crypto. No agency accepts bitcoin fines. No recovery firm that contacts you first is real.
- Before buying a small token: liquidity locked, contract cannot mint or freeze, top ten wallets hold a minority of supply. If you cannot verify, you cannot buy.
- Family rule: any urgent money request by phone or video is confirmed by calling back on a known number with a safe word.
- Full taxonomy in [scam-taxonomy.md](scam-taxonomy.md); general checklist in the main library's [red flags and scam checklist](../../10-checklists-and-templates/red-flags-and-scam-checklist.md).

### 8. Tax set-asides

- Every sale, swap, or spend of crypto is a taxable event in most jurisdictions, including crypto-to-crypto trades and, in many places, staking and airdrop income.
- On every realized gain, move the estimated tax (marginal rate for short-term, applicable rate for long-term) into a separate cash account immediately. Many 2021 and 2025 winners owed tax on gains that had evaporated by the time the bill arrived.
- Keep records of every transaction: date, amount, cost basis, fees. Use tracking software from day one; reconstructing five years of exchange history is miserable and expensive.
- Details in this library's crypto tax section: [../08-tax-and-regulation/](../08-tax-and-regulation/).

### 9. What to do in a crash

Follow the main [market crash playbook](../../10-checklists-and-templates/market-crash-playbook.md) and [handling market crashes](../../09-behavioral-finance/handling-market-crashes.md), with these crypto-specific additions:

- Do nothing for 48 hours except verify that your exchanges are processing withdrawals. If any is not, withdraw from the others now.
- No adding to altcoins during a cascade. Ever. The average top-100 token wicked roughly 58% intraday on October 10, 2025 and most never recovered those highs.
- If the crash pushes the allocation below half its target, add on the next scheduled rebalance date, from cash you had planned for it, never from borrowing or emergency funds.
- Do not check prices overnight. Nothing you do at 3 a.m. will be good.
- Expect a 50–90% drawdown to take one to three years to recover, if it recovers. Decide now whether you can hold that long; if not, your allocation is too big.
- Watch for the second wave: contagion through lenders and exchanges usually arrives weeks after the price crash (Terra in May 2022 became 3AC in June, Celsius and Voyager in July, FTX in November).

## The one-page version

Print this, sign it, and put it where you will see it.

**CRYPTO RULES (signed: ________ date: ________)**

1. Total crypto is ____% of net worth (max I can lose entirely). Core BTC/ETH ≥ 80% of that. Speculative ≤ 20%.
2. No leverage. No margin. No perps. No borrowing against it. No exceptions.
3. Long-term holdings in self-custody or an ETF. Exchange balances are a trading float only, never more than one month's expenses, never more than 20% of my crypto on one venue.
4. Seed phrase: offline, two places, never typed into a website, never given to anyone.
5. Yield only when I can explain the source in one sentence without the words "trading," "bot," or "strategy."
6. Rebalance quarterly. Above 1.5x target: sell to target. Any speculative position up 3x: sell the original stake. Gains go to boring assets.
7. Never increase the target because the price rose.
8. Unsolicited contact is a scam. I never sign a transaction I did not initiate. No giveaways, no recovery services, no urgency.
9. Tax on every realized gain goes to a separate cash account the same day. Records from day one.
10. In a crash: 48 hours of nothing except checking withdrawals; no altcoin buying; add only at the scheduled rebalance with planned cash; no prices after 10 p.m.
11. New purchases wait 72 hours from the impulse. No trades on weekends or between 10 p.m. and 8 a.m.
12. I read the best critics of what I own at least once per cycle.

## Countermeasures / How to apply

- Fill in the blanks on the one-page version today, before the next rally or crash, and store a copy with your investment policy statement.
- Set calendar reminders for the quarterly rebalance and the annual review.
- Automate what you can: scheduled purchases, withdrawal schedules, price alerts only at the rebalance thresholds.
- Share the rules with someone who will hold you to them; social accountability beats willpower.
- When you break a rule (you will), write down what happened and what it cost. That log is the most valuable document you will own in the next cycle.

## Key takeaways

- Size for total loss; a low single-digit share of net worth for most people.
- Zero leverage; it is the single largest cause of retail ruin.
- Self-custody or ETF for the core; exchanges hold a trading float only.
- No yield without a one-sentence, deposit-independent explanation.
- Mechanical take-profits and rebalancing are the only reliable top-and-bottom signals.
- Scam defense is a process: second-channel verification, no unsolicited offers, no unrequested signatures.
- Set aside tax on the day of every gain.
- In a crash, the first 48 hours are for checking withdrawals, not trading; contagion arrives weeks later.
