# Crypto Risk and Behavior

Crypto's returns have been extraordinary in both directions, and the losing direction is where most people actually end up. This section is the library's honest accounting of that side of the ledger: the exchanges that vanished with customer money, the "stable" coins that went to zero in a week, the scam industry that now takes an estimated $14–17 billion a year (Chainalysis, 2025 data), and the psychology that keeps pulling new buyers in at the top. It is not an argument against owning crypto. It is the material you should read *before* you decide how much to own, where to keep it, and what rules you will follow when the market is doing something frightening. Every file ends with countermeasures and takeaways; the last file compresses all of them into a one-page rule set. Figures are date-stamped because this space changes fast. Cross-links point to the main library's behavioral-finance and crash-playbook files, which apply here with extra force.
